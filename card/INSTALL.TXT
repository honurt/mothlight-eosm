MOTHLIGHT for the Canon EOS M  --  install in 3 steps
=====================================================

Needs: the original EOS M (2012) on Canon firmware 2.0.2, a fast UHS-I card,
and a charged battery. Back up the card first: step 1 erases it.

1. FORMAT THE CARD IN THE CAMERA
   Menu > Setup > Format card.

2. COPY EVERYTHING FROM THIS FOLDER TO THE CARD
   autoexec.bin, ML-SETUP.FIR and the ML folder, at the top of the card
   (not inside another folder). Eject the card properly.

3. RUN THE INSTALLER ONCE
   Camera in photo mode (P, Tv, Av or M), then
   Menu > Setup > Firmware Ver. 2.0.2 > update.
   A plain screen titled "Magic Lantern install" appears. That is expected:
   it is the Magic Lantern installer that Mothlight is built on. It makes
   the card bootable and says "Please restart your camera" when done.
   Switch the camera off and on.

THEN
   Switch to movie mode. After a few seconds Mothlight shows its Welcome
   page with a setup check. Press SET on "Start shooting".

   INFO ............ quick bar: shutter, aperture, ISO, WB, focus, profile
   SET ............. next tile        UP / DOWN ..... change the value
   Tap the picture . focus there (Crop Mood presets: at x10, then back)
   Two-finger tap .. the menu; its first tab is Mothlight (Settings first)

GOOD TO KNOW
 - Canon's firmware is never replaced. Hold SET while switching on to
   start plain Canon.
 - Never delete autoexec.bin from this card and keep using it: format
   the card in the camera instead.
 - If the camera ever freezes, take the battery out.
 - Full guide: https://github.com/honurt/mothlight-eosm/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md

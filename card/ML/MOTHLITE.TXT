Mothlight - Magic Lantern for the original Canon EOS M (firmware 2.0.2)
by Onur  -  https://github.com/honurt/mothlight-eosm

Loaded at start: crop_rec (Crop Mood), mlv_lite, mlv_snd, mlv_play, sd_uhs, moth_ui, file_man.
Turn modules on or off in the ML menu, Modules tab, then restart the camera.

In movie mode, press INFO to open the Mothlight profile panel.
Your own profiles go in ML/SETTINGS/PROFILES.TXT (see the GitHub page for the format).

Based on Magic Lantern (magiclantern.fm) and Crop Mood by Bilal Fakhouri. GPL-2.0.

Mothlight - Magic Lantern for the original Canon EOS M (firmware 2.0.2)
by Onur  -  https://github.com/honurt/mothlight-eosm

In movie mode:
  INFO            the quick bar (shutter, aperture, ISO, WB, focus, profile, settings)
  MENU tile       Mothlight settings; Settings > About > Welcome and setup check
  PROFILE tile    recording profiles; Settings > Save Crop Mood's settings as a profile
  Two-finger tap  Magic Lantern's full menu

Loaded at start: crop_rec (Crop Mood), mlv_lite, mlv_snd, mlv_play, sd_uhs, moth_ui, file_man.
Your own profiles live in ML/SETTINGS/PROFILES.TXT (see docs/PROFILES.md for the format).

# The Mothlight name and logo

The **code** in this repository is free software under the GNU GPL v2 (see `LICENSE`).
You may use, study, change and share it, including in forks, under the terms of that license.

The **name "Mothlight"** and the **Mothlight logo** (the iris monogram in `art/`) are not
licensed under the GPL. They identify this project and its author, Onur.

## What you can do without asking

- Say your project is "based on Mothlight" or "a fork of Mothlight", with a link back.
- Use the name to talk about Mothlight: reviews, tutorials, videos, forum posts.
- Keep the name in unchanged builds you pass on for free.

## What needs a different name

- A fork with changes that you distribute: please give it its own name and logo, and
  remove "Mothlight" from the camera's menus and boot screen.
- Anything you sell, or bundle with something you sell (cameras, cards, rigs, courses).
  Selling GPL software is allowed by the GPL, but not under this name or logo.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue on GitHub.

This is a statement of intent, not legal advice.
